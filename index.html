01 / Introduction

A little about me

I'm Yongchao Zhu, a Computer Science master's student at Université Paris-Saclay. I completed the first year of the Quantum and Distributed Computer Science (QDCS) program in 2025–2026 and am continuing my M2 studies in 2026–2027.

Before that, I earned a bachelor's degree in Network Engineering from Zhongyuan University of Technology, China (2020–2024). My undergraduate work combined software engineering, networking and operating systems, including a thesis on visualizing operating-system algorithms.

I enjoy understanding how software behaves beneath the surface: how programs interact with the operating system, how computation can be distributed or parallelized, and how careful implementation improves performance.
